# 20 Lessons From the PowerShell Podcast, Whether I Learned Them or Not

## Abstract

After hundreds of episodes of the PowerShell Podcast, I have learned a few things. Some were technical. Some were career-changing. Some were deeply embarrassing. Most were learned live, in public, with a microphone on.

In this Poshakucha, I’ll speedrun 20 lessons from hosting the PowerShell Podcast: what I’ve learned from guests, community members, awkward moments, brilliant conversations, technical rabbit holes, and the occasional recording disaster. Expect a mix of practical advice, honest reflection, behind-the-scenes stories, and a few things I probably should have figured out much earlier.
