# A PowerShell Adventurer’s Field Notes

## Abstract

Not every PowerShell adventure ends with a module release or a thousand-line script. Sometimes the best rewards are the small treasures you discover along the way.

Join me for a fast-paced lightning demo where I’ll share a collection of PowerShell gems I’ve picked up during my travels: extending hashtables with GetValueOrDefault through type data, using ValidateSet and enums without setting traps for your future self, and letting parameter set names guide your functions through the right path.

Think of it as five minutes of loot drops for your next PowerShell expedition.
