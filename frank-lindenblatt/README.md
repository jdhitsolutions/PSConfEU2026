## Frank Lindenblatt

*Senior DevOps-Engineer at Finanz Informatik GmbH*

After my first contact with the legendary C64 in 1984, I began my professional career in IT in 1989 with MS DOS and Novell Netware.
In 1993, I moved to Finanz Informatik as a systems programmer, initially on the mainframe and OS/2.
Around 2009, I switched platforms to Windows and have been programming in C# and Powershell as well as Linux with C ever since.
My current role is best described as Senior DevOps Engineer.
