## Michael Herman

*Decentralized Systems Architect*

Michael is a 40+ year veteran of the computer industry with a Master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Waterloo. Michael has worked for Microsoft, IBM, and a number of Microsoft partners. Currently, he is a Decentralized Systems Architect at the Web 7.0 foundation.
