## Jeffrey Snover

*Harvard Fellow / Philosopher-Errant aka ‘The Heretic of Redmond’*

Jeffrey Snover is a Fellow at Harvard’s Berkman Klein Center. He is retired and spends his time as a Philosopher-Errant, attending science and technology conferences and giving public talks. Prior to retiring in 2026, he was a Distinguished Engineer at Google and a Technical Fellow at Microsoft where he was an AI Architect in Office, the Chief Architect for Windows Server Azure Stack. Snover is the inventor of Windows PowerShell, an object-based distributed automation engine, scripting language, and command line shell.
