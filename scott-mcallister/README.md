## Scott McAllister

*Software Developer at Patch My PC | Turning coffee and beer into enterprise patching solutions.*

Scott writes code for Patch My PC, which is a polite way of saying he turns coffee and beer into automation tools for ConfigMgr and Intune. He loves digging into the weeds of .NET and Microsoft Graph to figure out how things work—and how to make them work better. The creator of the MEM Patching Optimizer, Scott is always looking for ways to eliminate manual IT tasks. He lives in Scotland, where his two dogs remain completely unimpressed by his PowerShell scripts.
